# 腾空剑气

第 3 镜，约 4 秒。腾空、剑气或气劲从上往下。

等级 A 可以整句搬。等级 B 只作打法参考。衔接块不填打戏镜头。

## C1-3

- 来源：C1
- 等级：A
- 打法：以小打大
- 风格：3D 仙侠
- 五样：运镜、招式、后果、特效、节奏（5/5）
- 字数：589

广角低机位加上升摇臂；从巨兽肩头蹬上夜空，翻身红弧，锥形剑气轰在头背，盔甲连环爆炸碎片掉落，镜头震动

```text
At 00:04.000, the camera cuts to a wide-angle low angle with a high-altitude rising crane shot. <Subject 1> launches off the beast's shoulder into the stormy sky in one fluid motion, no lingering in mid-air. Her body flips crisply, and the Tachi cuts an extremely sharp scarlet arc. She releases a cone-shaped crimson sword qi storm that bombards the beast's head and back from above. Chain explosions burst across its armor, chunks of shadow plating tear off and fall, the ground below cracks open again, high-frequency camera shake, intense and violent impact, rapid rhythm without drag.
```
