# 落地对峙

第 6 镜，约 12 秒。落地对峙。

等级 A 可以整句搬。等级 B 只作打法参考。衔接块不填打戏镜头。

## 日漫A2-6

擂台广角：砸出大坑，两人弹开各蹲坑两边，主角怒视、反派不再笑

```text
At 00:12.000, the camera cuts to a wide low-angle shot on the arena, stable framing. The two fighters crash down onto the arena in a single impact, a ring of shattered ground and dust exploding outward and blasting a huge smoking crater through the middle of the white stone ring. Out of the dust they spring apart and land in low crouches on opposite sides of the smoking crater, <Subject 1> on the left with his golden aura burning, <Subject 4> on the right with his yellow-green aura burning, his armor cracked and dusty; <Subject 1> glares with fierce anger and <Subject 4> is no longer smiling, both staring each other down under the blue sky.
```

## 日漫B4-6

拉远大全景；少年在左朝右，平静得可怕，手垂在身侧；反派在右朝左退半步，额头一滴汗，又硬撑起笑摆架势；少年慢慢迈一步，脚下石头裂开

```text
At 00:12.000, the camera cuts to a slow pull-back wide establishing shot, stable framing. <Subject 1> stands on the left facing right, utterly calm, arms loose at his sides, lightning flickering over his aura. <Subject 4>, on the right facing left, takes half a step back, a bead of sweat sliding down his white face, then forces his smirk back and raises his guard. <Subject 1> takes one slow step forward; the cracked stone under his foot instantly splits and a blue-white spark leaps across it, the supersonic crack of his aura echoing over the wasteland.
```

## 日漫C1-6

拉远大全景；主角在左甩手吹红手掌，刺客在右掸西装、铁色褪去，鸟落回肩上；两人重摆架势

```text
At 00:12.000, the camera cuts to a slow pull-back wide establishing shot, stable framing. <Subject 1> on the left hops and shakes his stinging hands, blowing on his red palms; <Subject 4> on the right, untouched, brushes a speck of dust off his black suit as his iron sheen fades. Dust drifts across the cracked rooftop between them, the broken pillar and collapsed wall behind <Subject 4>, as the two instantly snap back into fighting stances under the sunset.
```

## 日漫C2-6

拉远大全景；主角在左站在墙洞里捏指节、压帽子，刺客在右捡帽子戴上、整领带，鸟落肩

```text
At 00:12.000, the camera cuts to a slow pull-back wide establishing shot, stable framing. <Subject 1> stands up in the jagged hole of the broken wall on the left, cracks his knuckles and settles his hat; <Subject 4> on the right picks up his hat, puts it back on and straightens his tie. Dust and small stones are still falling from the broken wall, sparks of sunset light flickering across the cracked rooftop between them.
```

## 日漫C3-6

拉远大全景；主角在左冒蒸汽喘气，刺客在右从冒烟的坑边慢慢站起，西装破了嘴角流血，眼睛开始发光、肌肉鼓胀（为下一段豹人变身铺垫）

```text
At 00:12.000, the camera cuts to a slow pull-back wide establishing shot, stable framing. <Subject 1> on the left, steaming and panting, in a low stance; <Subject 4> on the right rises slowly from the edge of the smoking crater, suit torn, blood at his lip; his eyes begin to glow and his muscles start to swell and bulge beneath his torn suit, the stone cracking beneath his feet as a dark impact wave ripples out from him.
```
