# 词句零件

从等级 A 的成片提示词里拆出的句子。整句搬，只换人名、武器、颜色和场景物件。★ 是 16 个招牌片段，提交检查按识别片段计数。

这些句子和识别名只用于挑选和自检，不要把本页标题抄进提示词。

## 运镜

12 条，其中招牌 5 条。

### extreme low-angle

- 中文：极低角度，从静止特写起
- 适合：开场第一镜；下一秒接爆发
- 招牌：否

```text
Extreme low-angle camera starting with a static close-up of <Subject 1>.
```

### tracking shot

- 中文：动态跟拍（环绕两人）
- 适合：近身快打；一对一时加 circling
- 招牌：否

```text
the camera cuts to a dynamic tracking shot (circling <Subject 1> and <Subject 4>).
```

### side-tracking

- 中文：贴地快速侧跟
- 适合：贴地三连斩这一镜；一段里最多用一次，别整段锁侧面
- 招牌：否

```text
the camera cuts to a fast low side-tracking shot.
```

### ★ crane shot

- 中文：广角低机位加高空俯冲摇臂
- 适合：腾空、剑气从上往下轰；一起跃起用 rising crane shot
- 招牌：是

```text
the camera cuts to a wide-angle low angle with a high-altitude diving crane shot.
```

### ★ orbits 360

- 中文：360 度快速环绕后切脸部特写
- 适合：每段一次，放在第 7 秒左右；整段唯一的脸
- 招牌：是

```text
the camera orbits 360 degrees rapidly around <Subject 1>, then instantly cuts to a facial close-up.
```

### cold close-up

- 中文：冷脸特写锁住脸，眼睛扫视
- 适合：紧跟 360 环绕；只写眼神，不写细微表情
- 招牌：否

```text
Cold close-up locking on her face, eyes scanning enemies.
```

### ★ vertical rapid falling

- 中文：垂直下坠镜头，定格在冲击瞬间
- 适合：硬接、下劈、制胜一击
- 招牌：是

```text
the camera cuts to a vertical rapid falling shot, freezing at the moment of impact.
```

### ★ freezes on a close-up

- 中文：定格刀身特写，刀光占满画面，杀意爆发
- 适合：开场拔刀；一对一改成两刀相撞特写定格
- 招牌：是

```text
the camera freezes on a close-up of the blade: a sweep of crimson metallic glow dominates the frame, signifying an explosion of killing intent.
```

### ★ pull-back wide

- 中文：缓慢拉远大全景，构图稳定
- 适合：收尾最后一镜
- 招牌：是

```text
the camera cuts to a slow pull-back wide establishing shot, stable framing.
```

### high-angle

- 中文：从人物身后往下俯拍大全景
- 适合：空中放大招、能量对撞
- 招牌：否

```text
the camera cuts to a high-angle wide shot looking down past <Subject 1> toward the clouds.
```

### falling alongside

- 中文：空中跟着两人一起下坠的跟拍
- 适合：空中近身快打
- 招牌：否

```text
the camera cuts to a tracking shot falling alongside the two fighters in mid-air.
```

### spiraling push-in

- 中文：快速螺旋推进
- 适合：段尾再次冲撞、锁刀（k3b 用过）
- 招牌：否

```text
the camera cuts to a fast spiraling push-in as they rush at each other again.
```

## 招式

15 条，其中招牌 5 条。

### ★ supersonic

- 中文：一秒内从静止瞬间超音速冲出
- 适合：开场爆发；一对一写 both burst forward at the same instant in a supersonic charge
- 招牌：是

```text
Within the first 1 second, she instantly bursts from a still state into a supersonic dash.
```

### ★ zero lag

- 中文：零延迟闪开 / 零延迟一一格开，每次格挡溅火花
- 适合：近身快打的防守；一对一用 deflects，别用 grazing her body
- 招牌：是

```text
<Subject 1> dodges with zero lag; deflects each one with zero lag, every block throwing a spray of sparks.
```

### ★ afterimage

- 中文：贴地冲刺留下多道残影
- 适合：反击前的近身
- 招牌：是

```text
She dashes low to the ground, leaving multiple afterimages.
```

### ★ three ultra-high-speed

- 中文：三连超高速斩：横斩、上挑、旋斩
- 适合：反击；一定写出三个具体招式名
- 招牌：是

```text
She executes three ultra-high-speed strikes with her Tachi—horizontal slash, upward thrust, spinning cut.
```

### no lingering

- 中文：一气呵成蹬地腾空，不在空中停留
- 适合：腾空
- 招牌：否

```text
<Subject 1> pushes off the ground into the air in one fluid motion, no lingering in mid-air.
```

### flips crisply

- 中文：利落翻身，刀划出锋利红弧
- 适合：腾空后放剑气前
- 招牌：否

```text
Her body flips crisply, and the Tachi cuts an extremely sharp scarlet arc.
```

### pivots in mid-air

- 中文：空中转身闪避，接超音速近身突刺，像箭穿阵
- 适合：特写之后的突进
- 招牌：否

```text
She pivots in mid-air to dodge, seamlessly transitioning into a supersonic close-range thrust, piercing through the enemy formation like an arrow.
```

### mid-air direction

- 中文：蹬墙借力、空中变向、瞬移冲刺
- 适合：炫身法；一打多
- 招牌：否

```text
Extreme agility display: wall-kicking for leverage, mid-air direction changes, teleport-like dashes.
```

### ★ dissipates the force

- 中文：硬接重击，瞬间卸力反击
- 适合：硬接反击；配垂直下坠定格
- 招牌：是

```text
<Subject 1> hard-blocks a heavy strike, instantly dissipates the force and counters.
```

### bends backward

- 中文：下腰毫厘躲过，焰浪擦脸削断几缕头发
- 适合：闪避；打空的招要砍到身后环境
- 招牌：否

```text
She bends backward under it by a hair, the flame wave sweeping just over her face and severing a few strands of silver hair.
```

### near the hilt

- 中文：旋身砍在剑柄附近把剑打飞，剑旋转插进某物
- 适合：缴械；写清打的是剑不是人（C3 验证有效）
- 招牌：否

```text
She spins and strikes his blade near the hilt, knocking the jian out of his hand; it spins through the air and stabs into <道具>.
```

### blades lock

- 中文：两刀相抵磨刃，慢动作，红紫光脸对脸
- 适合：一对一快打中间的停顿
- 招牌：否

```text
Brief slow-motion as their blades lock and grind against each other, crimson and purple light pressing face to face.
```

### pass each other in mid-air

- 中文：空中交错三次，每次相撞荡开一圈冲击波
- 适合：空中对决
- 招牌：否

```text
They pass each other in mid-air three times, blades crashing, each clash sending a ring of shockwave rippling outward through the clouds below.
```

### like a crimson arrow

- 中文：头朝下像红箭一样俯冲刺过去
- 适合：空中压下去的一击；对方要写成横剑挡住
- 招牌：否

```text
<Subject 1> dives at <Subject 4> head first like a crimson arrow, her Tachi thrust forward at supersonic speed.
```

### at the same instant

- 中文：两人同时爆发冲向对方
- 适合：一对一开场或再次冲撞
- 招牌：否

```text
Both burst forward at the same instant in a supersonic charge.
```

## 后果

10 条，其中招牌 2 条。

### ★ chain reaction

- 中文：脚下地面连锁塌陷，碎石尘土被高速气流喷出
- 适合：冲刺；“快”画不出来，后果画得出来
- 招牌：是

```text
the ground beneath her feet cracks and collapses continuously in a chain reaction, with debris and dust violently ejecting outward due to high-speed airflow.
```

### two chains

- 中文：石砖裂成两串相向而来，尘土落叶炸开
- 适合：一对一对冲
- 招牌：否

```text
the stone tiles crack and lift in two chains racing toward each other, dust and leaves blasting outward.
```

### ★ wind pressure

- 中文：风压把头发衣摆拉直，刀锋般的剪影
- 适合：冲刺、开场
- 招牌：是

```text
Her silver-white long hair and the hem of her white robe are pulled straight back by wind pressure, creating a sharp, knife-edge silhouette.
```

### fragments

- 中文：盔甲被撕成碎片，火花四溅
- 适合：砍中敌兵/怪物（不要用在要活着的对手身上）
- 招牌：否

```text
tearing enemy armor into fragments with dense sparks flying.
```

### chain explosions

- 中文：地面连环爆炸
- 适合：剑气落地
- 招牌：否

```text
The ground undergoes chain explosions.
```

### in a ring beneath

- 中文：脚下地面环形开裂
- 适合：硬接
- 招牌：否

```text
the ground cracking in a ring beneath her feet.
```

### gouging

- 中文：滑退拖出长长火花，脚跟在石头上犁出两道沟
- 适合：被震退（一对一打不死的对手用这个代替砍中）
- 招牌：否

```text
He is pushed sliding backward across the tiles in a long trail of sparks, his heels gouging two grooves in the stone.
```

### sliced cleanly in half

- 中文：身后石柱被整齐劈成两半，滑开砸地扬尘
- 适合：打空的招砍到环境；先确认这件道具后面不用
- 招牌：否

```text
a stone pillar behind him is sliced cleanly in half and slides apart, crashing onto the floor in a burst of dust.
```

### shockwave

- 中文：看得见的冲击波荡开
- 适合：相撞、钟声、落地
- 招牌：否

```text
a visible shockwave rippling across the terrace.
```

### lands heavily

- 中文：重重落地，尘土碎石散开
- 适合：收尾落地
- 招牌：否

```text
<Subject 1> lands heavily, dust and debris scattering.
```
